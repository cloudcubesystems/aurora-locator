Aurora Locator

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Applies to Aurora Locator 2.0 and later on Android and iOS

Aurora Locator is made by CloudCube Systems Inc, Fredericton, New Brunswick, Canada (“we”). The short version: no ads, no account, no tracking, and we have no server that receives your data.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone’s location while the app is open (never in the background) to work out aurora visibility, cloud cover, darkness and moonlight where you are. You can refuse and type a place name instead.

  • To get the cloud forecast, your coordinates, rounded to about 1 km, are sent over an encrypted connection to Open-Meteo. “Better spots nearby” sends points rounded to about 10 km. Nothing else is sent with them: no name, no device ID, no account.
  • The town name shown in the app comes from your phone’s built-in geocoding service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS).
  • Your last location, saved places and settings are stored only on your phone.

Place search

When you search for a place, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service to find matching places.

Space weather data

Aurora, Kp, solar wind and alert data are downloaded from the U.S. NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center. These requests contain no information about you or your location.

Notifications

Aurora alerts are optional. Your phone checks the forecast in the background and creates the notification itself; no push server is involved and nothing is sent to us.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. We only see the anonymous, aggregated statistics that Google Play and the App Store provide to every developer (installs, crashes, ratings).
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.

App store features

The “rate this app” prompt and update prompts use Google Play’s and Apple’s own built-in services, which are covered by Google’s and Apple’s privacy policies.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo: privacy and terms
  • NOAA SWPC: privacy policy

Deleting your data

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything the app stored on your phone, clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
